About

All your feedback is automatically saved.

Some principles we thought about when making this site:

  • We're trying to write clear technical documentation. To do that, it's more important to us to hear from learners about what you don't understand (and what they want to know!) than from experts about what they think people should hear.
  • We store as little personal information as possible: just whatever name you enter.

This site was made by Marie Clare LeBlanc Flanagan and Julia Evans for Wizard Zines. It's heavily inspired by Help This Book.
